# Red Team Objections

These are adversarial questions a real buyer would ask after reading the startup's homepage. For each audit, identify which objections the current copy answers and which it leaves unaddressed. Unaddressed objections are conversion blockers.

Use the role-specific sections to simulate each buyer persona's mental state when evaluating the startup.

---

## CEO / Founder

The CEO is asking: Is this worth my time and budget? Will it actually move the needle?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "Why would I pay for this when I could use ChatGPT myself?"
- "How do I know this company can actually deliver, not just talk about AI?"
- "What happens if the AI they build doesn't work as promised after 3 months?"
- "How is this different from what my existing IT vendor offers?"
- "What's the ROI? Not 'efficiency gains' — give me a number."
- "Why now? Why not wait 12 months when AI tools are cheaper and more capable?"
- "Have you done this for a company like mine? I need a real reference."
- "What's my risk if I invest $X and it doesn't work?"
- "Why should I bet on this startup vs. a large consulting firm that's been here for years?"

---

## COO / Head of Operations

The COO is asking: Will this actually integrate with how we work? Who runs this after you're gone?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "My operations team is not technical. Who trains them and how long does it take?"
- "How does this integrate with [our existing ERP/CRM/tool]? We have a complex stack."
- "What happens when something breaks at 11pm and my team needs it to work?"
- "Every vendor says 'minimal disruption.' What does implementation actually look like day-to-day?"
- "We tried to implement RPA 2 years ago. It failed. Why would this be different?"
- "Who maintains this after the project ends? We don't have an AI team."
- "What if the process changes 6 months from now? Can we adapt the AI without you?"
- "How long until we see actual results, not just 'progress'?"
- "My team is already stretched. How much of their time does this require?"

---

## CMO / Head of Marketing

The CMO is asking: Does this company's positioning make sense? Is there a market for this?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "Who exactly is this for? The site doesn't make that clear."
- "What's the ICP? I can't tell if this is for companies like mine."
- "Why would someone choose you over [named competitor]?"
- "The messaging is vague. What do you actually do in plain language?"
- "Your site says you use 'AI-powered solutions.' Every company says that. What's different?"
- "What does the customer journey look like from first contact to live system?"
- "Where are the case studies? I can't evaluate without seeing results."
- "What's your go-to-market approach? You seem to be targeting everyone."

---

## CTO / Head of Technology

The CTO is asking: Can these people actually build it? Will it be maintainable? Is it a security risk?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "What tech stack do you build on? Are we locked into specific vendors?"
- "What are the data privacy implications? Where does our data go?"
- "Do you use third-party AI APIs? If OpenAI changes terms or pricing, what happens?"
- "Who on your team has actually shipped production AI systems, not demos?"
- "How do you handle model drift, hallucinations, or edge cases?"
- "What's the maintenance burden on my team after delivery?"
- "What documentation do you provide? Code comments? Architecture diagrams?"
- "Have you ever built anything similar to what we need? Can I talk to a reference CTO?"
- "Is this proprietary tooling or standard frameworks my team can understand?"

---

## CFO / Finance

The CFO is asking: Can I justify this budget? What's the payback period?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "What does this cost, total cost including implementation, training, and ongoing maintenance?"
- "What's the payback period? If I spend $30K, when do I see $30K in savings?"
- "How do you measure success? What's the metric I report to the board?"
- "What are the ongoing costs after the initial engagement?"
- "What's the risk if we stop halfway? What have we paid for?"
- "Can we start smaller to test before committing to the full budget?"
- "Do you have pricing on your website? Why not?"
- "How does this compare to hiring a full-time data analyst or operations person?"

---

## Head of Sales

The Head of Sales is asking: Will this help us close more revenue? Does the company seem trustworthy enough to introduce to our clients?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "Can AI actually help our sales process or is this just hype?"
- "What specific sales use cases have you implemented? Not hypotheticals — real deployments."
- "How long from start to a live AI tool that my sales team actually uses?"
- "My sales team resists new tools. How do you handle adoption?"
- "We already have HubSpot/Salesforce. Does this plug in or replace something?"
- "What's the cost per rep per month if we scale this to 30 salespeople?"

---

## Head of Operations

The Head of Operations is asking: Is this going to create more work for me, or less?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "Tell me specifically which process this replaces and how many hours it saves per week."
- "What do I do when the AI produces wrong outputs? What's the escalation path?"
- "We have compliance requirements. How does this handle exceptions that need human approval?"
- "Who do I call when it breaks? What's the SLA?"
- "My team will be skeptical. How do you build buy-in with frontline staff?"
- "Is there a pilot we can run for 30 days before committing?"

---

## Skeptical founder (of another company)

The skeptical founder is asking: Is this just another AI agency cashing in on hype?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "Every month there's a new 'AI agency' with no track record. What's yours?"
- "You've been in business for [X months]. Why should I trust you over someone with more history?"
- "What makes your methodology different from just hiring a couple of Upwork AI contractors?"
- "I've seen companies like yours take the money, build something mediocre, and disappear. What's your guarantee?"
- "What AI applications have you actually shipped that are still running 12 months later?"

---

## Investor / Operator reviewing credibility

The investor is asking: Is this a real business with a real moat, or a services shop pretending to be a product?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "What's your competitive moat? Anyone can start an AI consulting firm."
- "What's your revenue run rate? Client concentration?"
- "What's your delivery methodology? Is it documented or all in the founders' heads?"
- "How do you scale delivery beyond 2–3 consultants?"
- "What percentage of your clients expand or renew after the first engagement?"
- "Why does this market need another AI agency?"
- "What would it take for a larger firm to eat your lunch?"
- "Are you building toward a product, or staying services-only indefinitely?"

---

## Technical buyer

The technical buyer is asking: Can I trust this company's technical claims?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "Your site says 'AI agents' — do you mean function calling, RAG pipelines, or something else?"
- "Which LLMs do you use and why? Are you model-agnostic?"
- "What's your approach to evaluation and quality control before going live?"
- "What testing frameworks do you use to prevent AI-generated errors?"
- "How do you handle PII in your data pipelines?"
- "What happens when OpenAI deprecates a model version you've built on?"
- "Do you have any open-source contributions or public technical content I can evaluate?"

---

## Non-technical buyer

The non-technical buyer is asking: Can I actually understand and trust this?

**Adversarial questions:**
- "I don't understand what an 'AI agent' is. Can you explain what this actually does for me?"
- "Will I need a technical person on my team to manage this?"
- "What if I don't understand the outputs the AI produces? How do I know it's right?"
- "I've heard AI makes things up. How do I know it won't make mistakes that cost me money?"
- "My employees are afraid AI will replace them. How do I handle that?"
- "Is this something I could have done with just ChatGPT for free?"
- "What does 'implementation' actually mean in plain English?"
- "I'm not very technical. Will your team speak to me like a real person or use jargon?"
