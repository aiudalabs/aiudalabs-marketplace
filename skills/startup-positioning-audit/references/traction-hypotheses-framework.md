# Traction Hypotheses Framework

Before a startup launches, they need hypotheses about how customers will find them. This framework evaluates which acquisition channels are realistic at the current stage and ICP, which offers will generate leads, and what to experiment with first.

The goal is not to recommend every possible channel — it's to prioritize ruthlessly and identify what will likely fail before the startup wastes 3 months on it.

---

## 1. Likely acquisition channels (by stage and ICP)

### Outbound (most effective for B2B services at early stage)

**Cold outreach via LinkedIn DM / email:**
- Best for: B2B services targeting identifiable buyer roles (COO, Operations Director, CTO of 50–500 person companies)
- Requires: Clear ICP, specific pain message, credible LinkedIn/company presence
- Success signals: 5–15% response rate to well-targeted messages
- Failure signals: Generic messages to vague ICP, no social proof to reference

**WhatsApp outreach (LATAM-specific):**
- High-signal for LATAM B2B sales, especially SME market
- Requires: Personal introduction or mutual connection context
- Risk: Cold WhatsApp without context is spam

### Content / SEO (medium-term, 3–12 months)

**Long-form content targeting search intent:**
- "How to automate [specific process] with AI for [industry]"
- Best for: Buyers in early awareness or consideration stage
- LATAM Spanish-language content is significantly under-supplied — major white space
- Requires: Sustained publication cadence, 6+ months to see meaningful traffic

**LinkedIn content (founders posting):**
- Most effective: Founder shares case study results, specific implementation stories, contrarian views on AI adoption
- Failure mode: Generic "AI is transforming business" posts — no differentiation, no engagement
- Time to first lead: 4–12 weeks of consistent posting

### Partnerships and channels

**Technology partnerships (AI tool vendors):**
- AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure all have partner programs for AI implementation firms
- Partner programs provide referrals, co-marketing, and credibility
- Best for: Companies with 2+ deployments to showcase
- LATAM-specific: Hyperscaler LATAM teams are often looking for local partners

**Complementary service firms:**
- ERP consultants, accounting firms, HR software companies — all have clients who need AI without being AI specialists themselves
- Referral agreement: They refer, you deliver, everyone wins

**University and accelerator connections:**
- For B2B AI in LATAM: Strong partnerships with startup ecosystems (Endeavor, NXTP, Platzi community, university AI programs) can drive early credibility and referrals

### Events and workshops

**Free public workshops:**
- "AI for Operations Teams" — half-day free workshop attracts operations managers
- Converts well when followed by a diagnostic offer
- Can be done in person (especially in LATAM for trust-building) or online

**Paid workshops or training days:**
- Generate revenue + qualify interested companies simultaneously
- Best positioning: "The workshop is the product; implementation is optional."

### Inbound from social proof

**Case study-driven inbound:**
- When a client publicly shares results, inbound referrals follow
- Requires: At least 1 documented case study with specific numbers
- Best amplification: Client posts their own success story on LinkedIn, company shares it

**Referrals (word of mouth):**
- Highest conversion rate of any channel
- Requires: 2–3 completed engagements with happy clients
- Active referral program vs. passive: Consider asking explicitly, not just hoping

## 2. Offers that may generate leads (by channel)

| Offer | Best channel | Conversion likelihood | Why |
|-------|-------------|----------------------|-----|
| Free AI Readiness Audit | Outbound, LinkedIn | High | Clear value, low commitment |
| Free workshop | LinkedIn events, partnerships | Medium-High | Attracts interested buyers, builds trust |
| Published case study | SEO, LinkedIn | Medium | Attracts similarity-matched buyers |
| Free diagnostic report | Outbound | High | Specific deliverable, low friction |
| Webinar: "AI ROI for [industry]" | LinkedIn, email list | Medium | Educates + qualifies |
| Productized sprint (visible price) | SEO, content | Medium | Self-selects buyers with budget |

---

## 3. Offers unlikely to convert (at early stage)

| Offer | Why it won't work |
|-------|------------------|
| "Book a call" as primary CTA | Not an offer — too vague, no value exchange |
| Enterprise pricing page only | Self-disqualifies the SME buyers most likely to convert early |
| Course/content without relationships | SEO takes 6+ months; no proof yet = no trust |
| "Subscribe to newsletter" cold | No existing audience = no signups |
| "Partnership inquiry" form | Zero urgency, no clear value for the partner |
| Paid ads targeting vague "AI for business" | CAC will be unsustainable without very specific ICP and offer |

## 4. Pre-launch experiments (90-day priority)

These are the experiments worth running in the first 90 days to validate channel + offer combinations:

**Experiment 1: LinkedIn founder content (Week 1–12)**
- Post 3x/week: case studies, opinions, specific implementation stories
- Goal: 10 meaningful DMs from target ICP profiles
- Failure threshold: <5 relevant engagements after 4 weeks → pivot message

**Experiment 2: Outbound LinkedIn sequence (Week 2–6)**
- Target 50 specific buyer profiles (COOs, Operations Directors in target verticals)
- Message: Reference a specific pain, offer a free diagnostic
- Goal: 5 discovery calls booked
- Failure threshold: <5% response rate → rewrite message

**Experiment 3: Free workshop (Week 4–8)**
- Host 1 in-person or virtual workshop for target ICP
- 15–30 attendees goal
- Offer: Post-workshop free diagnostic for attending companies
- Goal: 3 diagnostic engagements from 1 workshop

**Experiment 4: 1 anchor case study (Week 1–6)**
- Find 1 past client or pilot client willing to do a documented case study
- Write with specific numbers: before/after metrics, time frame, ROI
- Publish on website and LinkedIn
- Goal: 5+ inbound inquiries in 60 days post-publish

## 5. Content wedges

Content that creates a specific claim to expertise and attracts SEO + social traffic:

**High-value content for B2B AI companies (LATAM):**
- "The 5 AI Use Cases with the Highest ROI for Logistics Companies in Brazil" — vertical + geography + specific use case
- "Why 70% of Internal AI Projects Fail (And How to Build the Other 30%)" — addresses a common pain
- "How to Calculate the ROI of AI Automation Before You Build Anything" — practical, attracts decision-makers
- "The AI Readiness Checklist for Operations Teams" — gated or ungated lead magnet

**What to avoid:**
- "The Future of AI in Business" — too broad, no competitive search angle
- "Top 10 AI Tools You Should Know" — already overdone, no differentiation

---

## 6. Partnership wedges

Partners who provide warm referrals or joint credibility:

- ERP/CRM implementation partners (SAP, Salesforce, HubSpot resellers who need AI layer)
- Cloud provider partner programs (AWS/GCP/Azure partner for LATAM)
- Industry associations (logistics, manufacturing, financial services in target geography)
- Startup accelerators and incubators who need AI education for portfolio companies
- Executive education programs at business schools

---

## 7. Community wedges

- LinkedIn groups for target vertical + role
- WhatsApp groups for operations managers in target industries (LATAM-specific)
- Slack communities for founders or ops professionals
- Spanish-language AI practitioner communities (Platzi community, etc.)

---

## 8. Outbound wedges

- Direct LinkedIn outreach to named ICP titles at identified target companies
- Re-engagement of past contacts who expressed AI interest
- Conference follow-up sequences (after attending relevant events)
- Personal network activation (founder's existing professional relationships)

---

## 9. Event/workshop wedges

- Industry-specific "AI for [Vertical]" workshops
- Lunch-and-learn for a mid-market company's leadership team
- Speaking at regional tech/business events
- Hosting "AI Office Hours" for free 20-minute consultations

## 10. Metrics to track per channel

| Channel | Metric | Target (90 days) |
|---------|--------|-----------------|
| LinkedIn content | Profile views from ICP | 500+/week |
| Outbound DM | Response rate | 10–20% |
| Workshop | Attendees | 20+ |
| Workshop | Post-event diagnostics booked | 3+ |
| Website | Unique visitors | Track baseline |
| Website | CTA click rate | >3% |
| Inbound leads | Per week | 1–3 |
| Discoveries booked | Per month | 4–8 |
| Proposals sent | Per month | 2–4 |
| Close rate (proposal to signed) | Per quarter | 30–50% |
